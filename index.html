About

I am an Economics undergraduate at Universidad Panamericana in Mexico City, expecting to graduate in December 2026. My research interests are inflation dynamics, causal inference, and household health.

My thesis studies Mexico’s Phillips curve under the supervision of Dr. Arnulfo Rodríguez Hernández at BBVA Research. I also work as a research assistant to Professor Akito Kamei on household water treatment and child health, using survey microdata and double/debiased machine learning.

Research

The Phillips Curve in Mexico: Economic Slack, Time-Varying Parameters, and Forecasting

2026–present

Undergraduate thesis · In progress
Advisor: Dr. Arnulfo Rodríguez Hernández

I compare measures of economic slack and constant versus time-varying parameters to study inflation dynamics. The empirical work uses Kalman filtering, nonlinear least squares, and regression methods.

Household water treatment and child health

Aug 2026–present

Research Assistant to Professor Akito Kamei · In progress

Using UNICEF MICS microdata to study household water treatment, E. coli contamination, and child diarrhea. My work includes double/debiased machine learning for average and group-specific effects, specification checks, and sensitivity analysis for potential unobserved confounding.
